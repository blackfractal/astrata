# Answer key — spoilers

## Preferred route

**North, north: pre-empt Shard-Walker.** Take the initiative against its 9 Earth opening attack, then accept Dervish's less costly Wind ambush after the first battle and rewards are resolved.

The key is to evaluate both encounters as a sequence, not just select the more dangerous enemy in isolation.

| First engagement | Follow-up geometry | Follow-up opening hit with current equipment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shard-Walker, at (4,7) | Dervish is a Hunter and reaches you wherever you stand | 8 Wind; Earth Bronze absorbs 3; **5 HP lost** |
| Dervish, at (3,10) | Shard-Walker steps (3,8), (3,9), (3,10), reaching you in its three-space move | 9 Earth; Earth Bronze absorbs 2; **7 HP lost** |

Coordinates above are one-based. Neither Eidolon is currently Restless. Entering an occupied space spends the rest of that movement turn, so engaging Dervish in one step does not preserve a second escape step. Fighting an enemy you walk onto lets you place cards first. An enemy landing on you attacks before placement. The remaining Eidolon moves after the first encounter/reward flow, before the next spawn pair; simply winning the first fight does not grant another movement turn.

The **verified immediate ambush difference is 2 HP** with current equipment. The broader benefit of arranging defenses before Shard-Walker's opening attack and entering Dervish with another reward depends on the actual play and draws. Do not label an unverified larger full-fight HP swing as guaranteed.

## Position and deck support

- Shard-first ends at (4,7), four clear northward movement steps from the Tavern at (4,3).
- Dervish-first ends at (3,10), at least seven movement steps from the Tavern. These are geometric distances, not guaranteed safe travel or arrival times; future encounters can intervene.
- Earth bracelet is strong against Dervish's Wind, neutral against Shard-Walker's Earth.
- Sapling and Earth-attuned Shields can offer effective Wind defense once placement is available. Dervish's third move is a three-hit Whirl, so prepare sustained defense rather than treating the cheap opening as the whole fight.
- Shard-Walker later negates an attack with Reflective Shield and uses a 12 Earth piercing Lance. Pre-emption does not make that battle trivial.
- The Ruby is currently loose. Its presence does not provide a Fire-attuned bracelet; socketing requires the Jeweler. Focus Draught can help establish the first battle's board, subject to its normal use rules.

Jonathan and Codex agreed Shard-first has a clear preference from the currently visible equipment and positions. This is not a proof across all draw orders, rewards or combat decisions. Dervish-first has a quantifiable immediate disadvantage and worse Tavern positioning; a player proposing it should explain what compensating benefit they expect.

## Assessment rubric

- Identifies both follow-up Eidolon encounters as forced by their movement from these positions, assuming the first fight is won and normal encounter flow continues.
- Distinguishes pre-emptive player initiative from enemy-first ambush.
- Correctly computes Dervish 5 HP versus Shard-Walker 7 HP after the bracelet.
- Notices that the one-step Dervish engagement forfeits the second movement step.
- Includes the northward Tavern route, rather than assessing combat alone.
- Recognizes current Restlessness, loose versus socketed Gems, and the limitations of a currently empty battle grid.
- Separates the known immediate two-HP advantage from uncertain later combat outcomes.
- Does not inspect future RNG or draws to decide the route.
