An overwhelmingly frightening apex hunter about to spring directly toward the viewer from a rocky mountain ledge. Strong value contrast makes the head and teeth visible: warm lava rim light from below, cold crystal rim light from above, deep indigo shadows, restrained luminous violet around edges. Frightening and alien without gore. No boar, no tusks, no bison, no human, no rider, no armor, no mechanical parts, no text, no letters, no logo, no watermark, no UI or card frame.
