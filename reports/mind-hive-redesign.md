# Mind Hive redesign — 3.0.6

Jonathan requested a 15-round procession of mixed-element insects hatched from space Corruptions, replacing the direct-spawn waves that largely failed in his first Hive fight. His original victory remains archived: 27 HP entering, 31 leaving after Holy Armor, no damage taken. Storm Canopy now explicitly excludes Ideons; other area attacks keep their normal targets.

The approved rules are recorded in the main design and S3 planning. First tuning: two eggs in the rightmost column on rounds 1–3, two in two columns on 4–6, three in three columns on 7–10, four in four columns on 11–14. Round 15 resolves existing commands and hatches before the timer expires. Eggs can land beneath existing cards and stay fixed. Uncovered eggs deal 8 of their shown element through normal positional defenses; covered eggs freely discard the entire Construct, including Allies, and remove its stored Shield. Both hatch an insect at the same space. Eggs can be removed immediately with Machine Elves; newborn insects wait until next enemy turn to act. Existing Ideons delay hatching on their space instead of creating overlaps. Other Corruptions/Bile and Ideons exclude new egg placement. Owner death cleans up its eggs and insects.

Routine implementation choices: seeded uniform choice among legal band spaces; a seeded shuffled seven-element cycle guarantees different elements within a batch; alternating 16-HP/12-damage Burrowers and 10-HP/9-damage Skitters retain their existing movement traits. Arcane is included. These are tuning defaults, not a claim of final balance. No artificial damage is inflicted for failed placement. No additional one-turn warning precedes egg placement because the egg itself is the full-turn warning.
