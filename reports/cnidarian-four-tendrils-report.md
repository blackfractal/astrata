# Cnidarian: fourth starting Tendril

2026-10-25 — rules/content/package 4.0.32, design 4.253.

Jonathan approved increasing the Cnidarian's opening from three Tendrils to four after his no-HP-loss victory. New encounters, including Apex rematches, now begin with four distinct owned Tendrils. HP, damage, pursuit, replacement and scaling rules are unchanged. Existing battles retain their saved Ideons; Continue does not add a Tendril retroactively.

Validation: all 797 rules tests pass. New regression coverage checks both encounter initiators, unique unoccupied spawn spaces, ownership, 18 base HP, Continue preservation and older three-Tendril saves. No new balance playthrough was performed, so the difficulty effect remains to be assessed in play.

The live profile was not edited. Restart the application to load the updated build before starting a new encounter.

Tavern review: the inspected post-S3 save has 207 Gold and already owns Fury Sap. Mothlight Crown (70) plus Prismatic Tincture (110) costs 180, leaving 27. Sturdy Metal Helmet (75) is an alternative but only buffs cards with Shield in their name. Mirror Armor offers one full reflected attack per battle; Armor of Remembrance instead grants 2 temporary player Guard per destroyed card, capped at 8 per round. Recommended retaining Mirror for the Apex Predator, and keeping Remembrance as an optional spare. No purchases or equipment changes were made.
